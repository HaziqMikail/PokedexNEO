## type resist

Damage Multipliers Explained
4x (Double Weakness): Dealt when both of a defender's dual types are weak to an attack.

2x (Super Effective): Dealt when an attack is effective against a single-type defender or against one of a defender's two types.

1x (Neutral): Standard damage.

0.5x (Not Very Effective / Resistance): Damage cut in half.

0.25x (Double Resistance): Damage cut to a quarter when both of a defender's dual types resist an attack.

0x (Immunity): Completely blocks all damage regardless of second typing.
